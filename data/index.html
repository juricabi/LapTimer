Home WiFi

Join the timer to your home WiFi, or leave empty to use its own hotspot. Save, then restart to apply.

Restarting into the hotspot. In a few seconds join LapTimer_xxxx 192.168.4.1 (password laptimer) and open http://192.168.4.1.

  • On your WiFi open http://laptimer.local, or the timer's IP from your router.
  • WiFi not found within 60 s of power-on: the timer starts its own hotspot LapTimer_xxxx 192.168.4.1, password laptimer.
  • Once connected it stays on your WiFi and reconnects if the WiFi drops.
