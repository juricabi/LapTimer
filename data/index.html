WiFi networks

When it powers on, the timer joins the strongest saved network in range. If none is in range, it starts its own hotspot.

Restarting into the hotspot. In a few seconds join LapTimer_xxxx 192.168.4.1 (password laptimer) and open http://192.168.4.1.

  • On your WiFi open http://laptimer.local, or the timer's IP from your router.
  • No saved network in range (or it can't join within 60 s): the timer starts its own hotspot LapTimer_xxxx 192.168.4.1, password laptimer.
  • New networks are used after the next restart. Passwords stay on the timer.
